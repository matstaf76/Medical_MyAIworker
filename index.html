Privacy by Deliberate Design

Support your safeguards.
Respect the boundary.

Morgan can be configured to support HIPAA-conscious workflows, including minimum-necessary data collection, approved disclosures, access controls, secure integrations, documented escalation, and human oversight.

Responsible implementation means:

  • Map where protected health information may enter the workflow.
  • Use eligible vendors and appropriate agreements where required.
  • Limit Morgan to approved administrative tasks and responses.
  • Escalate emergencies, symptoms, treatment questions, and sensitive decisions to people.
  • Review the full deployment with your privacy, security, and legal advisors.

No blanket compliance promise: HIPAA and other obligations depend on your full environment—technology, contracts, policies, training, integrations, and daily use. No single AI tool makes an organization compliant.

Straight Answers

What healthcare offices need to know.

Does Morgan diagnose, triage, or give medical advice?

No. Symptoms, emergencies, diagnoses, treatment, prescriptions, and other clinical decisions are escalated to qualified people according to your protocols.

Is Morgan HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Morgan is a fully HIPAA-compliant AI medical administrator. That compliance applies specifically to Morgan and the system we provide; it does not certify or guarantee the compliance of your practice's other technology, vendors, policies, staff, or operations.

What does Morgan replace?

Morgan reduces repetitive administrative load and missed opportunities—not licensed clinicians or staff accountability for patient care.

How does monthly billing work?

Service is $1,500 per month for the first user and $1,000 for each additional user. The first invoice is sent 30 days after go-live. There is no monthly checkout here.
