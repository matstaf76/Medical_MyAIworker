Straight Answers

What Modern Law Firms Need to Know

Is this meant to replace lawyers?

No. Legal.MyAIworker.online automates client engagement and routine practice workflows so attorneys can work faster while retaining legal judgment, strategy, and final review.

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. It takes down the caller's information, screens for conflicts, tells them plainly that no attorney-client relationship exists until your firm accepts the matter, and hands them to you. If a caller pushes for an opinion on their case, it says that's a question for the attorney and gets them to you instead.

Is the email chatbot included?

Yes. Voice reception, website chat, and an email chatbot are included in the service. It can answer approved administrative questions, gather preliminary intake details, guide prospects toward booking, and escalate sensitive messages to your team.

What does the Labor Day Special include?

Am I locked into a contract?

Who do I reach if something breaks?

A real person in the US. Support is human, not a ticket queue, and additional hands-on remote work is billed at $95/hr if you ever want it.

One honest note. No vendor can make your practice compliant on its own — compliance depends on how your office operates as a whole. What we do is make sure the AI answering your phones is built, papered, and configured so it strengthens your posture instead of quietly undermining it.
