Effective September 1, 2026

Do not share private patient information in the public demo.

Morgan is a public demonstration with no live access to patient records. Do not submit protected health information, medical details, Social Security numbers, payment card details, financial account information, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Medical.MyAIworker.online and the public Morgan demonstration, provided by MyAIworker.online operated by SomeTechDude.com. A separately implemented customer system may have additional agreements, notices, safeguards, and retention requirements.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

Demo conversations are not used to build marketing lists. Email is used to respond unless the sender later becomes a customer or separately agrees to additional communication.

Service Providers and HIPAA

We do not sell personal information. Necessary third parties may process technical, session, communication, hosting, AI, voice, form, scheduling, or payment information to provide the site and requested services. Stripe processes payment card information under its own privacy terms.

HIPAA obligations depend on the full environment, including configuration, eligible vendors, required agreements, access controls, policies, training, integrations, and actual use. The public demo is not a patient intake channel and should never receive real patient information.

Retention and Choices

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purposes above, legal obligations, security, disputes, and customer service. Retention periods vary by provider and record type.
